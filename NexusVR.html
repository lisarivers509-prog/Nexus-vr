NexusVR

Stream your gaming PC’s screen to any headset browser or low-end laptop. The heavy lifting stays on the host; the viewer only decodes video and sends back pointer and head movement.

Host: the gaming PC

Run this page on the machine that runs Steam. Share the whole screen, or the SteamVR mirror window.

Viewer: headset or laptop

Open this page on the Quest browser, a laptop, or any WebXR device. Paste the host’s code.

Not connected

Viewer settings

Changes apply live. In VR: thumbstick up/down moves the screen away or closer, left/right resizes it, grip recenters it in front of you.

Everything here is peer-to-peer (WebRTC): video and audio from host to viewer, and pointer clicks plus head and controller pose back over a data channel. Both devices should be on the same network. Passing the pointer to the host game, or head tracking into SteamVR itself, needs a small native helper on the host, because browsers cannot control other programs.
